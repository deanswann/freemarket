About FreeMarket

FreeMarket is an independent play-money prediction platform built around a simple idea: make a prediction, put virtual points behind it, and track how your calls perform over time.

What it is

Markets cover topics such as sports, economics, technology, politics, culture and financial markets. Each market starts with an estimated probability and can move as users make predictions.

What it is not

FreeMarket does not accept deposits, offer withdrawals, award cash prizes or give virtual points any monetary value. It is designed for forecasting and entertainment, not gambling or investing.

Questions or feedback?

FreeMarket is still evolving. Features, market rules and presentation may change as the platform improves.
