How Probora works

Each market asks a YES/NO question with a clear resolution rule and closing time.

Trading

  • Choose YES or NO and stake virtual points.
  • Your entry price is based on the current market probability.
  • If your prediction is correct at resolution, your payout is based on the shares you received when entering.
  • Open positions may be cashed out while the market is still open; a 2% cash-out fee applies.

Probabilities

Markets begin with an opening estimate and synthetic starting liquidity. Real user activity then moves the displayed odds. Opening probabilities are informed estimates, not guarantees.

Resolution

Each market includes a source and resolution rule. Trading closes before the outcome is meant to become known. Once the result is confirmed, the market resolves YES or NO and eligible positions settle.

Play-money only.

Virtual points cannot be bought, sold, transferred, withdrawn or redeemed for money or prizes.
