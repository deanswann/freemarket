FreeMarket

Privacy

This page describes the current FreeMarket prototype at a high level. It should be reviewed and expanded before a broad public or commercial launch.

Account data

If you create an account, Firebase Authentication processes the email address and authentication information needed to sign you in. FreeMarket stores account-related game data such as your virtual balance, positions and profile settings in Firebase/Firestore.

Public profile data

Profile and leaderboard information is only shown publicly where the relevant public-profile or leaderboard setting allows it. Email addresses and internal account identifiers are not intended to be displayed publicly.

Technical data

Hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information required to deliver and secure the service, such as request, device, security or diagnostic data.

Purpose

Data is used to provide account login, synchronize game state, operate markets, prevent abuse, maintain security and improve the service.

Important

FreeMarket is still under development. Before a wider public launch, this privacy notice should be completed with the operator identity, contact details, applicable retention periods, legal bases and any other disclosures required for the final deployment and audience.
